**E-COMMERCE SALES ANALYSIS**
**Project Overview**
This project analyses e-commerce sales data to uncover business insights, evaluate sales performance, and identify trends across product categories, regions, and payment methods.

**Objectives**
-Evaluate overall revenue and profitability.
-Identify top-performing product categories.
-Analyse regional sales performance.
-Examine customer purchasing behaviour.
-Investigate product returns and payment prefrences.

**Tools Used**
-Microsoft Excel
-Pivot Tables
-Excel Charts
-Interactive Dashboard

**Key Metrics**
-Total Revenue: $5,865,293
-Total Orders; 34,500
-Total customers: 7,903
Profit: $970,019.41

**Key Findings**
-Electronics generated the highest revenue
-Fashion recorded the highest number of orders.
-The south region contributed the highest revenue share.
-Credit cards were the most preferred payment method.

**Project Deliverables**
-Cleaned dataset
-Pivot table analysis
-Interactive dashboard
-Business insights and findings

**Conclusion**
The analysis provides insights into sales performance, customer behaviour, regional contributions, and product category performance to support better business decisions.
